title:          "Fault-free analogue computing with imperfect hardware"
date:           2026-06-12 00:00:00 +0800

pub:            "Nature Electronics"
pub_date:       "2026"
abstract: >-
  Analogue in-memory computing computes directly within memory, but device failures and variations
  limit its precision and reliability. We report a fault-free matrix representation that decomposes
  any target matrix into two adaptive submatrices programmed onto the analogue hardware, allowing
  optimization to bypass faulty devices. Our memristor-based system achieves a cosine similarity of
  over 99.999% for a discrete Fourier transform matrix despite a 39% device fault rate.
